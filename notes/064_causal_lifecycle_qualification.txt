Causal lifecycle qualification across provider extraction
=========================================================
2026-11-08 | Design direction; implementation checklist: note 065

Decision and scope
------------------
Qualify concurrent ownership under uncertain termination in provider-core.
Use causally controlled external services to qualify each real provider adapter.
As the common service is extracted, move its broad behavioral tests with its
implementation. Preserve a small set of external consumer scenarios across that
move; changing service assembly should not require rewriting their assertions.

This note refines the testing approach in 058 and the extraction roadmap in 060.
Their historical qualification records, and the candidate/publication limits in
061, remain evidence for their named artifacts only. In particular, replace
elapsed-outage scenario control with lifecycle prerequisites; qualify traffic,
Retry-After and deadlines separately. The new work does not require another
runtime abstraction, another publisher, or an extraction of scientific policy.

The companion 065 is the actionable roadmap. This note records source review and
design, not a new test run, release qualification or deployment. Existing causal
fixes in Magnet are part of the baseline, not tasks to implement again.

Ownership now and after extraction
----------------------------------
1. provider-core owns transport/process lifetime, Interpreter, AttemptController,
   AttemptClaims, execution permission, delivery custody and publication. Their
   component contracts and composed ownership exploration remain in this repo.
2. The providers currently own announcement, discovery, admission, service-level
   controller retention, pacing, maintenance and drain. Under note 060 those
   common mechanics move into provider-core, with their broad test matrices.
3. Providers declare offerings, generation/eligibility rules, resource sharing,
   readiness and continuation policy, and construct the real AnalysisSession.
   They retain scientific inputs/tools, preparation effects, accepted handles,
   scientific retry, native protocol admission and result/failure meaning.
4. Existing native binding tests retain physical ownership evidence. Generic
   RunnerSession/AsyncRunnerSession mechanisms stay with their SDK owner;
   provider-specific stream, source-reader and warm-reuse obligations stay with
   the adapter until a concrete common mechanism is separately extracted.
5. Deployment/identity/image tooling has its own shared extraction boundary.
   It does not become a dependency of the lifecycle state-machine harness.

The common service calls the adapter. An adapter does not implement another Job
poller or drain loop. No mandatory class hierarchy or provider-name branches are
needed. One process-local registry excludes the same Job across its lanes and
generations; it is not a distributed lock or permission inferred from inventory.

Model obligations, not a single ready flag
------------------------------------------
Keep the following facts distinct in the model and journal:

- Server acceptance of START; parent receipt of matching execution permission.
- Receipt of HTTP bytes; parent classification of that exchange's result.
- Selected input candidate; retained interpreter result; accepted session-owned
  PreparedAnalysis or native validation capability; scientific effect entry.
- Model exchange retirement; scientific operation quiescence; worker retirement;
  source/grant/scratch release; terminal disposition; full claim release.
- Local durable handoff acceptance; final Jobs API publication receipt; queue
  removal. An accepted relay report does not establish either physical cleanup
  or final publication.
- shutdown_ready and claim release. A safely passive unresolved identity can
  remain excluded without preventing supported shutdown; an unaccepted selected
  report or active physical obligation cannot be silently treated that way.

SupervisedChatTransport owns one active exchange. Concurrent operations use
independent transport/interpreter/controller owners and the appropriate shared
claims registry. ProseService can deliberately share backoff for one binding;
that intended pressure is not an isolation failure. Keep announcement, discovery,
live observation, source and delivery exchanges independent when they must make
progress concurrently.

Track capacity as the resources actually reserved: live entries, control bytes
and pending terminal bytes. Delivery acceptance can release terminal pressure
while original lifecycle ownership remains. Binding an acknowledged Attempt
identity does not create another live entry. Registry capacity is not scientific
allocation readiness.

Safety, isolation and conditional progress
-----------------------------------------
S1. A successor uses an allocation only after the original owner establishes the
    required operation/handle quiescence or physical retirement. The evidence
    must match that exact resource and generation.
S2. An operation cannot release another operation's claim, transport, source,
    prepared handle, report or allocation.
S3. Cancellation, a closed socket, a sent signal, or observation-timeout expiry
    cannot turn unconfirmed retirement into confirmation.
S4. Expired or locally verified revoked permission cannot authorize a new
    scientific effect. The adapter checks permission at the actual effect,
    including after intervening awaits; a prior check reserves no authority.
S5. Accepted preparation and its original evidence remain owned if later cleanup
    fails. Retention does not authorize execution after permission is withdrawn.
S6. Recovery cannot repeat accepted or uncertain scientific work. Explicitly
    rejected candidates may be corrected; a provider's separately authorized
    scientific retry must satisfy its own retirement, identity and budget rules.
S7. An Attempt receives at most one logical selected terminal decision. Permitted
    transport retries retain the same operation, bytes and idempotency identity.
S8. Late events from an old owner cannot change successor state or evidence.
I1. An independent operation progresses when its required resources, permission,
    shared capacity and configured continuation policy permit it.
I2. Failure in one operation cannot corrupt another's deadline, status, claim,
    accepted preparation, report or delivery state.
L1. Original obligations can settle once their required cleanup evidence and an
    admissible execution/disposition decision are available.
L2. Released capacity permits eligible work when its scientific allocation and
    remaining admission conditions are ready.
L3. Shutdown completes once all required retirement and handoff duties settle,
    under the documented treatment of passive retained identities.

Liveness assumes the required external replies/exit observations eventually
arrive and the relevant tasks receive scheduling time. There is no unconditional
claim for an exit that can never be established or arbitrary CPU starvation.
Physical cleanup alone does not invent a public FAIL or settle uncertain START.

Provider differences that the oracle must preserve
--------------------------------------------------
SECS has one resident worker and stops admission on retained ownership. A healthy
operation can leave that worker quiescent for reuse; an abandoned prepared handle
or uncertain original request can require physical retirement before source
release. An explicit input_rejected response permits corrected preparation.
Count the exact accepted handle and its consumption, not all prepare calls.
Its production controller reads verified Job input after START; do not introduce
a second read to imitate another provider's preverified-input path.

NMRNet retains verified upload bytes, scientific readers, geometry generation,
native setup and prediction owners. Selected input is not yet an accepted SDK
handle. Its configured zero-or-one prediction retry is specific: a validated
ModelCallFailed reply, retired original worker, continuing permission, the same
frozen request batch, a matching replacement runtime, and the original aggregate
budget. A lost sent reply is unresolved and grants no retry. A cleanup-only proof
allocation is not a scientific submission. Preserve those distinctions.

NMRPeak has separate warm HF/CHF runners but shared discovery, live capacity and
delivery-binding pressure. A recognized completed and observed operation can
restore runner quiescence without killing that boot. Validation corrections after
an explicit rejection are legitimate; an unfinished original validation or an
accepted capability cannot be replayed. Typed input requires no model HTTP.
Current failure propagation can stop the binding. Broader lane continuation must
be an explicit behavior change when the concurrent service is introduced.

Magnet supplies heterogeneous scientific implementations and their readiness,
generation and failure policies. Its existing lane isolation is a consumer proof,
not a reason to put E01/E02/G01 or its current status-board API into the generic
state model. Shared admission/drain tests move with the extracted service.

Small causal testing support
---------------------------
Keep three responsibilities separate:

- Driver: chosen external actions, reply/ACK releases, stop/revocation events and
  controlled-time advances in the simulated layer.
- Journal: facts actually observed from the real subject and controlled peers.
- Oracle: an independent ledger of original obligations, accepted identities,
  permitted decisions and outstanding capacity. Do not copy the runtime's
  branching algorithm or derive expected state from its mutable status alone.

An observation needs a kind, source, collector sequence, known operation/resource
identity and optionally a monotonic timestamp. Add an original-owner/generation
identity where late events can collide. Record only identities known at that
boundary; do not infer that the nth HTTP request belongs to the nth START.

A sequence counter orders collection, not independent processes. Causal edges
come from actual interactions: the original request precedes its response; the
parent classifies that response; a restoration gate depends on verified prior
settlement. Delayed log arrival is not evidence of when a child physically exited.

The shared capabilities are record, await a predicate, withhold/release an
external response, and report outstanding obligations. Keep the replayable action
trace separate from the observation journal. A failed example must identify its
actions, choices, boundaries, artifact and unsatisfied condition.

Record facts at their owners: API acceptance, interpreter classification,
session/native effect entry, SDK handoff acceptance, original controller join or
post-terminal settlement, and the actual registry. Prefer typed event observation
that delegates to the real emitter over parsing rendered log text. Logging is not
an authority for permission or release. Observers must not repair runtime state,
release claims, clear backoff or restart science to satisfy the test.

Watchdogs cancel only disposable waiters, not the operation being observed.
Preserve strong references to owners and check owner failure even when an event
arrives at the same wake-up. Capture the verdict and diagnostics before releasing
emergency fixture gates or terminating children. Cleanup contains failed tests;
its effects are not evidence that the tested lifecycle completed successfully.

Three complementary qualification layers
----------------------------------------
A. Composed state-machine exploration in the SDK.
   Start with two operation slots and small entry/byte capacities, using the real
   Interpreter, supervised transport ownership, controller/executor and claims.
   Replace controllable external process/pipe, API, science and delivery ports.
   Generate legal event orderings, not just random parameter values. Cover launch
   interruption, malformed/partial/lost replies, uncertain START, late cleanup,
   permission changes, accepted preparation, ACK loss and shutdown.

   Use Hypothesis with one managed event loop per example. Do not create a new
   loop per action and cancel the very owners being explored. Keep deterministic
   named traces as regression cases; shrink failing action sequences. Controlled
   time is appropriate only where all relevant simulated clock sources agree.
   This explores exposed boundaries, not every internal asyncio instruction.

   The first distinct new test consumer is the concurrent composed lifecycle
   suite. Reuse existing tests/fakes and unit/integration scenarios before adding
   helpers. The existing outbox history test is already stateful; it needs no
   replacement merely to adopt a different generator.

B. Small actual-process fault injection in the SDK.
   Extend tests/fakes/b3_provider_shutdown.py and its existing test with an
   original held operation plus an independently progressing operation. Reuse
   its creation, cancellation, signal, repeated-retirement and exact-exit gates.
   Keep real monotonic time, subprocesses and IPC. A dead child whose wait result
   is withheld tests unconfirmed observation, not a genuinely unkillable child.

C. Real provider/SDK composition and native/deployment qualification.
   Retain provider-owned scenario factories using actual adapters and production
   assembly paths. Qualify real native protocols separately from recording
   scientific backends, and deployed model continuity separately from scripted
   subprocesses. SDK process tests do not replace a consumer's worker binding.

The first composed witness holds A's original retirement, lets B complete and
settle using independent resources, then supplies A's original evidence. For a
positive release use an admissible path such as verified pre-entry revocation
and settle_revocation; also keep an unresolved-disposition control that stays
held after cleanup. Admit a successor only when its own prerequisites hold.
Retain a separate accepted-preparation witness; do not create a fictional next
model turn after ToolFinished has ended interpretation.

Component budgets remain distinct. A supervised chat exchange has its fixed
original retirement allowance. API/upload/delivery callers can own bounded
reobservation budgets. Do not turn either rule into a universal timeout policy.

Consumer scenarios that survive extraction
-------------------------------------------
Use existing fixture classes and test files. A provider scenario assembles the
real service/session, external peers, stop request and scientific observations.
During extraction change its service assembly, keeping Jobs, identities, command
entry, original handles, report bytes and settlement assertions stable.

SECS: extend test_sdk_runtime_drain.py, test_sdk_session.py, native_worker tests
and the existing end-to-end fixture. Prove safe failure then next-Job readiness;
accepted-handle/source lifetime through interruption; exact handoff through stop.
The reviewed e2e fixture manually builds ChatEndpoint with HttpTransport. Use the
production ChatEndpointFactory path for a supervised-child wiring claim. Its
pending durable queue row proves local custody, not final API publication.

NMRNet: reuse service_ports.py, runtime_ports.py, retirement_ports.py and native
session fixtures. Preserve verified bytes through interpreter interruption;
retain late native setup/cleanup under the original owner; preserve exact terminal
bytes under delivery pressure and stop. Keep scientific retry and result-format
tests local. Replace private task/call-sequence assertions when their owner moves.

NMRPeak: reuse test_shared_provider_process.py and test_native_runner_session.py.
Keep typed-input bypass and original validation/capability ownership, the durable
handoff-versus-cleanup distinction, and two-lane stop/ACK-loss scenarios. Today's
held-cleanup regression calls _select on the other lane; it does not prove that
lane completes under the running service. Add full independent completion at the
concurrent-service milestone under its explicit continuation policy. Preserve
warm-PID reuse and final native retirement proofs. The extra prose-stop tests
referenced by note 060 are on a test revision, not this main baseline.

Magnet: retain the current causal outage/finite-backlog cases and complete lane
isolation. Add shared observation/peer capabilities only where current consumers
need them. Replace remaining status-file polling with the actual hold boundary
and assert normal shutdown in the test body. The later heterogeneous deployment
witness has E02 held while independent E01 work and publication of G01's earlier
report progress; actual PIDs/model loads and API publication require that layer.

Do not broaden the matrix by pretending one-worker SECS has independent spare
allocations, by forcing fresh-child NMRNet to preserve warm PIDs, or by treating
NMRPeak's current binding stop as an already implemented lane-continuation policy.

Distribution and default execution
----------------------------------
The SDK source distribution already includes generic test helpers. NMRNet's
tools/provider_consumer_tests.py verifies that archive's checksum, copies an
explicit support allowlist, checks installed SDK provenance and excludes generic
SDK tests/source from consumer discovery. Extend this existing distribution path
once two actual external consumers use a shared deterministic helper.

Keep Hypothesis and shrinking private to SDK qualification, with a pinned
test-only dependency closure. Do not put it into production/native installs or
make providers import a sibling checkout. Runtime wheel, support archive and
provider revision are an exact qualified tuple, not just matching version text.
No additional test wheel, public runtime observer bus or generic orchestrator is
needed to begin. New files/targets/interfaces require their first real consumer.

Use a small default generated profile and an explicit longer campaign; note 065
sets initial budgets and verification gates. Extended run sizes and contention
counts are proposals until measured. Real-process tests retain real clocks.
Scenario gates determine fault start/end; dedicated timing tests verify deadlines
and Retry-After. Server request-arrival gaps alone cannot prove dispatch was not
early; observe the actual admission/dispatch boundary for that stronger claim.

Repetition is bounded and retains every failure. Diagnostic waits identify the
missing lifecycle transition; they do not retry until green or reset forever on
log chatter. Under declared load a real semantic deadline can legitimately fire.
Test outcomes must retain that distinction rather than increasing every timeout.

Migration and completion
------------------------
Follow SECS, NMRNet, NMRPeak, Magnet for service extraction. First qualify current
services against the selected SDK. For an extraction cut, exercise the old local
service and the new shared service against the same immutable candidate in
separate test environments. Preserve provider input/readiness/science policy.
Do not compare two live schedulers against the same provider identity.

Move announcement/pacing, then discovery/admission, then original-controller
retention/drain. NMRNet proves reusable maintenance of held duties; revisit them
even with full capacity, closed admission or no offerings. Concurrent providers
then prove resource- and binding-scoped pressure. Add deliberate new behavior,
such as fair discovery or broader continuation, with its own behavioral witness.

At each cut move the broad generic test matrix with its production owner and
delete the displaced local path. Keep the scientific/native tests and small
external scenarios. A runtime defect needs a deterministic witness and an owning
fix; an extraction of correct behavior starts under passing contract guards.

Before actual cutover close old admission and settle duties or verify durable
transfer to a continuing owner. A timeout is not a transfer of an in-memory
Attempt. Keep the current outbox and publisher authorities. Process death before
durable handoff remains note 060's separate series; a digest-only checkpoint does
not reconstruct selected report bytes and grants no scientific replay.

Completion means a generic lifecycle change normally changes the shared code
and its tests while the four consumer scenarios continue to validate their real
adapters. New providers supply science, declarations and a small qualification
fixture; they do not supply another polling, settlement or shutdown engine.
