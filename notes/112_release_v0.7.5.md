# provider-core 0.7.5

`JobService` acquires and prepares API Attempts concurrently within `LiveAttemptLimits`. Input bytes remain charged until their Attempt releases its claim; `maximum_preparations` limits concurrent session preparation. A full service retains its bounded feed page and waits for capacity. Slow science or delivery no longer serializes acquisition while capacity remains.

`BatchCall` reserves a `ProcessCall` and its privately owned worker, and collects prepared participants up to a configured size or collection deadline. Providers supply the entry codec and scientific pack/unpack functions. Borrowed `ScientificCall` objects are rejected because they cannot grant retirement authority over a shared host. Independent batches need separate process owners; invoke science through each owner and use its exposed host only for service health observation. The dispatcher retains an ordered participant snapshot and validates the complete reply before routing results; identical inputs remain distinct jobs. Queue limits include dispatched input until settlement. Direct calls, another dispatcher, and attachment during a call are rejected until successful close releases the reservation.

Permission is checked again immediately before scientific entry. Revoked queued participants are removed; cancellation after dispatch suppresses that job's newly selected publication but waits for shared settlement without stopping siblings. A sequential worker entry may still execute after its participant is revoked. Cancellation of all participants or global shutdown joins the original shared invocation. A verified server revocation can settle an already-entered Attempt after its session retires, without treating unrelated or forged failures as revocation.

Shared batches require `max_attempts=1`: malformed or lost whole replies are unresolved and never replayed. Only a confirmed pre-effect permission refusal permits repacking the remaining participants. A fatal batch failure retires the canonical host once; unconfirmed cleanup retains participation ownership and input charges. Already selected report duties remain owned by the original Attempts and delivery retries do not repeat science.

Configure `BatchPolicy` with maximum entries, collection seconds, queued input bytes and per-entry input bytes; enter the dispatcher after its scientific call starts and keep it alive through Attempt draining. `JobService.maximum_preparations` defaults to one, and `LiveAttemptLimits.maximum_input_bytes` defaults to 16 MiB. Existing scientific methods and direct-call providers need no batch schema change. Scientific batching, model parallelism and tensor batching remain provider decisions; ChefNMR2 uses sequential entries.

Qualification covers controlled admission, preparation, cancellation, routing and cleanup boundaries, including native worker exit after partial execution. Consumer image qualification is recorded separately in ChefNMR2. This release makes no live deployment or scientific accuracy claim. Repository convention findings remain recorded in `notes/092_repo_checks_status.html`.
