prevents accidental host execution; it does not attest to kernel containment.

and NMRNet-specific roots are removed from the local ownership inventory.

No hosted CI workflow is introduced; adding one changes the reviewed workflow
inventory and must pass the preserved action-pin/default/environment rules.

Validation policy
-----------------
Run the actual maintained Make lane offline. Distinguish detector-fixture or
port-wiring defects from product/convention findings and missing prerequisites.
Do not add skips, xfails, narrowed scan inventories or broad waivers merely to
obtain green. Report genuine reds with their owning rule. The checker's source
mounts inspect a live worktree; read-only does not freeze concurrent host edits.
