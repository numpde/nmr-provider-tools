Deferred wheelhouse work
========================

The joint deployment is a milestone, not completion of the shared wheelhouse
migration. Complete the provider-core dependency-download replacement, the
shared delivery streamed build and every provider's remaining runner and
worker migration. Qualify their normal cold paths before claiming completion.

The following items do not need to delay the current deployment:

- Shared delivery still uses a selected-file BuildKit named context against
  the live pool. Its eventual streamed context should remove the builder's
  pool access while preserving the existing service and storage.
- The provider-core repository's shared-publisher replacement needs a genuine
  missing-artifact cold-path qualification through its normal Make target.
- A wheel downloaded and discarded by a lock compiler for metadata may be
  fetched again by acquisition when the index lacks a metadata sidecar.
- Cache-aware projection uses current index metadata and excludes yanked
  candidates when non-yanked candidates exist. It cannot reuse an artifact
  absent from that metadata, and may prefer a non-yanked download to a cached
  yanked wheel. Explicit source and hash choices remain authoritative.
- The broad repository quality lane reports existing source-inventory,
  generated-file and policy findings alongside the wheelhouse changes. Triage
  those separately rather than expanding the provider deployment.

WLAN DNS failures during host-local acquisition remain an operational issue:
retain the failing diagnostic and use the configured interface and resolver.
Do not silently switch the download route to complete a deployment.
