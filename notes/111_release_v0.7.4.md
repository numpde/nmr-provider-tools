# provider-core 0.7.4

`ProviderClients` captures API identity, TLS/proxy settings and delivery publication material once, then creates separate exchange owners for each role. `termination_signals` installs and restores process stop handlers. `HelloPublisher` serializes complete offering snapshots, samples current offerings after each authorized retry, and retains withdrawal duty after an uncertain acknowledgement. Providers retain their catalog or availability policy; an optional retry callback receives the nominal delay and server minimum separately so its pacing policy can preserve the floor.

`JobService` now takes a tuple of `JobOffering` declarations associating each offering with its already-started `ProcessExecutionHost`, consumer, generation and session factory. Several offerings may share one host; a host loss withdraws every dependent availability offering while preserving affected Attempts. `HostFailurePolicy` explicitly chooses stopping or continuing on independent hosts. This service still executes jobs serially; it does not introduce cross-job batching. Enclosing provider scopes own startup policy and native host retirement.

`drain_attempts` stops and joins original controllers, then retains them until their work and selected-report duties permit shutdown. Cancellation and cleanup failures propagate after settlement; they cannot authorize discarding an unaccepted report. The controller owns eligible interrupted-result suppression. A permanently rejected report remains held without repeating science or automatically resubmitting the rejected report. Draining can remain pending indefinitely while a selected report is unsettled. Callers must keep native hosts, delivery clients and lifetime exclusion alive throughout draining.

Callers of the former single-offering `JobService` constructor move `offering`, `host`, `consumer_id`, `generation` and `new_session` into `JobOffering` entries supplied through `offerings`. They also supply `host_failure_policy` and `limits=LiveAttemptLimits(...)` from `provider_core.ownership`. The maintained examples validate all configured scientific models before starting any of them. Consumer qualification remains distinct from SDK qualification; no live provider deployment or scientific-model result is claimed. Repository quality findings remain recorded in `notes/092_repo_checks_status.html`.
