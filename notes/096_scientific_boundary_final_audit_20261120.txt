## Scope and meaning of the scores

The thirteen criteria and supported scientific profile are unchanged. A score
of 10 means the reviewer found no remaining actionable gap within that bounded
criterion after examining source and relevant executable evidence. It does not
mean exhaustive fault coverage or a guarantee that no undiscovered defect exists.

Repository checks remain enabled, unchanged, deferred and excluded. The explicit
PRIVACY_TESTS=0 campaign policy remains in force. Independent offerings,
real-model/GPU execution, broader concurrency, cross-version workers, controlled
replacement and unattended containment/recovery remain outside this audit.

The three independent read-only reviewers were asked to identify a concrete
trigger, consequence and smallest correction for every deduction, rather than
raise scores to meet the target. They clarified that their earlier half-point
deductions reserved conventional headroom; those deductions had not identified
unmet falsifiable requirements. This record preserves that distinction instead
of claiming new runtime improvements for a numerical reassessment.
